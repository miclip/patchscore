# The Show Me Love organ stab

`stab` · `bright` · 120 BPM (112–128) · A minor · 2 bars · 32 steps

## The technique

Land the chord on the "and" of every beat. Put a four-on-the-floor kick under it to hear the two interlock.

Keep it short: lift the hand before the next beat arrives, so the downbeat stays clear for the kick.

Accent the first stab of the phrase and play the rest evenly. That one accent marks where the two bars start.

Voice the three notes close together, above the bass and below the melody. A tight voicing is what makes it read as a stab.

## Where it plays

**Tracker Mini · Track 1, Track 2 and Track 3**

Single-note sample struck short, one note per track

This riff asks for a bright stab and the nearest this box authors is hard.

Stacked chord: 3 voices, one note each. Every voice takes the same settings below, so build the sound once and copy it across all 3.

Lowest note to the lowest voice: Track 1 takes the bottom of every chord and Track 3 the top. Hold that order and the voicing keeps its shape.

Source — A single-note tonal sample — one pitch, with a front edge. Yours, or one note rendered here; it does not need to be a chord and should not be one

**Trigger note** — `C5` · MIDI 60

Routing — Tracks 1-8 — costs no synth slot, and one loaded sample serves every track of the stack
